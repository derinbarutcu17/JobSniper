# Job Sniper

**Smart job sourcing, automated.**

Job Sniper helps you find the right opportunities without the noise. It scans job boards, company career pages, startup sources, and other public signals, then surfaces the roles and companies that actually fit your profile and preferences.

No doom-scrolling LinkedIn. No irrelevant recruiter spam. Just a focused stream of opportunities worth your time.

Job Sniper is in active development.

Join the waitlist: [jobsniper-waitlist.vercel.app](https://jobsniper-waitlist.vercel.app)

## What it does

- **Multi-source discovery** — pulls from job boards, ATS systems, startup databases, and RSS feeds in one pass
- **Profile-aware filtering** — scores and ranks roles against an actual profile, not just keywords
- **Company contact enrichment** — finds public company contacts, contact routes, and outreach-relevant details
- **Company contact research** — helps surface companies and the people or inboxes you can actually reach

## Why It Exists

Job hunting should be strategic, not exhausting. Job Sniper is built for people who want to find the right role, not all the roles. Fewer, better, faster.
